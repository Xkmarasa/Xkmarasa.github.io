Maquinaria / under the hood

Built to be trusted, not just used.

The database refuses double bookings, not an if.

A “check, then insert” breaks when two requests arrive at once. A date-range constraint in PostgreSQL means that of eight simultaneous bookings, exactly one wins.

Reviews are written blind.

Neither side sees the other's review until both are in (or 14 days pass), so the first rating can't anchor the second.

No shared deal, no conversation.

Messaging another company requires a listing or booking that links you, checked on the server. Locations are always rounded to a ~1 km grid.
