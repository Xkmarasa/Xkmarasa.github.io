1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information from one page to the next or between visits.

2. Which cookies we use

This website only uses its own cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work. We do not use analytics, advertising or social media cookies, and we do not share information about your browsing with third parties.

CookiePurposeTypeDuration
sb-…-auth-tokenKeeps you signed in and protects your account. It only exists if you sign in.First-party, technicalUntil you sign out (at most 400 days)

These cookies are exempt from consent because they are necessary to provide the service you request (art. 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI and the Spanish Data Protection Agency's guide on the use of cookies). That is why the website does not show a cookie banner.

3. Stripe payment pages

When you pay, we take you to Stripe's secure page, on its own domain. There Stripe uses its own cookies to process the payment and prevent fraud, under its cookie policy. We do not control them or receive their information.

4. How to manage or delete cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies from your browser's privacy settings. If you block technical cookies you will not be able to sign in or buy, but you can still read the public content.

5. Changes to this policy

If we ever add cookies that are not technical, such as analytics cookies, we will first ask for your consent with a notice that lets you accept or reject them just as easily, and we will update this policy.
