Valencia · Study centre

Holy Shroud Study Centre

Welcome to the CES. We research and share knowledge about the Shroud of Turin, the Holy Chalice of Valencia and the relics of the Passion with historical and scientific rigour, and with the respect owed to what many hold as an object of faith.

About us

What the CES is

The Holy Shroud Study Centre (CES, from its Spanish name) is an initiative based in Valencia that brings together people interested in the Shroud from different fields: history, archaeology, medicine, physics, chemistry, art and theology.

Our work is to gather and organise existing research, promote new studies and bring it all to the public in clear language, always distinguishing between what has been proven, what is debated and what belongs to tradition.

Valencia is a natural home for this task: since 1437 its cathedral has kept the Holy Chalice, one of the most venerated relics of the Passion in the world.

Our work

What we do

  1. 01

    Research

    We follow and promote studies on the cloth, its history and its image, using verifiable sources and methods.

  2. 02

    Share

    We publish a monthly magazine and accessible texts for anyone interested.

  3. 03

    Teach

    We organise meetings and talks for students, teachers and groups.

  4. 04

    Keep the memory

    We gather literature and documents on the relics of the Passion, especially those in Valencia.
