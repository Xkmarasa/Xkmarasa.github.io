Frequently asked questions

What happens if I cancel?+

You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that you can no longer download issues, including earlier ones.

How does renewal work?+

The subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year until you cancel it. Stripe sends you a receipt for every payment.

Why do I have to tick the immediate access box?+

Because you start reading as soon as you pay. In that case the law lets you waive the 14-day right of withdrawal, and we must ask you to accept that expressly.

Can I see an issue before subscribing?+

Yes. The sample issue can be downloaded for free from the magazine page.

Is the magazine printed?+

No, it is digital: each issue is downloaded as a PDF from your account.
