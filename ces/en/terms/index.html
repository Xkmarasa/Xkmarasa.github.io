1. Who we are and what these terms cover

These terms govern the relationship between [name of the association] (tax ID [tax ID], address [registered address], email [contact email]) and anyone who buys, subscribes or donates through this website. They apply together with the legal notice and the privacy policy.

We link to them before every payment and you can save or print them from this page. To enter into a contract you need an account and must be of legal age. The contract is concluded in Spanish or English, depending on the language you use on the website, and we keep it on our system.

2. Prices and taxes

Prices are shown in euros and include applicable taxes. Delivery charges, if any, are shown before you pay and added to the total. The price that applies is the one in force when you place the order.

3. How to make a purchase

  • Add the products to your cart and check the quantities.
  • At checkout, choose the delivery address and review the summary with the total price.
  • When you press the payment button, we take you to Stripe's secure page. The order is confirmed when Stripe confirms the payment to us; we then send you a confirmation email.
  • You can follow the status of your orders from your account.

We hold the items for 30 minutes while you pay. If payment is not completed in that time, the order is cancelled at no charge.

4. Payment

We accept the immediate payment methods offered by Stripe: cards and, depending on your device, digital wallets. Stripe handles payment data under the PCI DSS security standard; we never see it.

5. Delivery

We deliver orders to the address you provide. If it is outside our delivery area, the website will tell you before you pay. We will email you when the order is dispatched and when it is delivered.

The maximum delivery time is 30 calendar days from order confirmation. If we cannot meet it, we will let you know and you may cancel the order with a full refund.

6. Right of withdrawal for shop purchases

As a consumer, you have 14 calendar days to withdraw from the purchase without giving any reason, starting from the day you receive the product (or the last one, if they arrive separately).

To exercise it, tell us before the period ends with a clear statement, for example by writing to [contact email] or using the model form at the end of this page. Then return the product to us within 14 calendar days.

We will refund everything you paid, including standard delivery, within 14 calendar days of your withdrawal notice, using the same payment method. We may withhold the refund until we receive the product or proof that you have sent it. Return costs are borne by you, and you are liable for any loss in value of the product caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish its nature and functioning.

Under article 103 of the Spanish consolidated General Law for the Protection of Consumers and Users (TRLGDCU), there is no right of withdrawal for:

  • Products made to your specifications or clearly personalised.
  • Sealed products that cannot be returned for health or hygiene reasons once unsealed.
  • Sealed audio or video recordings and software that you have unsealed.
  • Single issues of newspapers and magazines, except subscriptions.

7. Product guarantee

All products carry the legal guarantee of conformity. If a product does not conform to what you bought, you may ask us to repair or replace it free of charge and, if that is not possible, for a price reduction or termination of the contract. We are liable for lack of conformity that appears within three years of delivery (art. 120 TRLGDCU).

Write to us at [contact email] with your order number and, if possible, a photo of the problem.

8. Digital magazine subscription

  • While active, the subscription gives access to the CES digital magazine: every published issue, to read or download as a PDF.
  • It can be monthly or yearly. It is charged when you subscribe and renews automatically at the end of each period for the same amount until you cancel. If we change the price, we will give you advance notice and you may cancel before it applies.
  • You can cancel at any time from your account. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid: until then you keep access and are not charged again.
  • If a renewal charge fails, we will email you so you can check your payment method. If it is not resolved, the subscription ends and access is withdrawn.
  • Right of withdrawal: in principle you have 14 calendar days from subscribing. However, as the magazine is digital content you access immediately, when you subscribe we ask you to expressly request that access starts straight away and to acknowledge that you therefore lose the right of withdrawal (art. 103.m TRLGDCU). We confirm this in the welcome email.
  • The guarantee of conformity for digital content lasts for as long as the subscription is active.

9. Donations

  • Donations are one-off and voluntary. They are paid through Stripe, but they are not a purchase: they do not entitle you to anything in return.
  • Every donation is identified: we ask for your name to issue the receipt and, if you want a certificate, your tax ID, which we also use to report the donation to the Spanish Tax Agency (form 182) where applicable.
  • You will receive a receipt by email, which is not an invoice.
  • As it is not a consumer contract, the right of withdrawal does not apply to donations. If you donated by mistake, for example the wrong amount, write to us at [contact email] within 14 days and we will look into it.

10. Customer service and complaints

You can send us any question, complaint or claim through the contact form or by writing to [contact email]. We will reply as soon as possible and within one month at the latest.

Official complaint forms are available: ask us by email and we will send them to you. You can also contact your local consumer office or the Consumer Arbitration Board.

11. Governing law and jurisdiction

These terms are governed by Spanish law, without prejudice to the rights granted to you by the law of your country of residence if you are a consumer in another EU Member State. The courts of your place of residence have jurisdiction over any dispute.
