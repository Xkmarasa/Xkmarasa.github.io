3. Data that may reveal religious beliefs

The CES is a religious association, so dealing with us (subscribing to the magazine, donating or buying) may reveal your beliefs. The GDPR treats such data as a special category (art. 9).

We process it in the course of the association's legitimate activities with people who have regular contact with it (art. 9.2.d GDPR), only for the purposes described and without disclosing it outside the association without your consent, unless required by law.

That is why we apply reinforced measures: access limited to those who need it for their work, a second authentication factor for administrators, a log of their actions and encryption in transit and at rest. The delivery team only sees the delivery name, address and phone number of each order.

4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell or share your data. To provide the service we use providers who process it on our behalf (processors), under contract and only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage.
  • Vercel: hosting and running the website.
  • Resend: sending service emails.
  • Sentry: detecting technical errors, without your email, IP address or form contents, which we remove before sending each error.
  • Our tax and accounting advisers and, where applicable, the e-invoicing service, to meet tax obligations.

Payments are processed by Stripe. You enter your card details directly on Stripe's page: we never see or store them. For fraud prevention and its own legal obligations, Stripe acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.

We also disclose data to the Spanish Tax Agency, courts and other authorities when the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Some of these providers are based in the United States or may access the data from there. In those cases the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified, or on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission (art. 46 GDPR), with additional measures such as encryption.

6. Your rights

You can exercise these rights at any time:

  • Access: find out what data of yours we process. You can download all of it as a file from your account.
  • Rectification: correct any inaccurate data. You can change most of it yourself from your account.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete it when it is no longer needed, except what the law requires us to keep.
  • Objection and restriction of processing, in the cases provided for in the GDPR.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw your consent where it is the basis for processing, without affecting earlier processing.

Write to us at [contact email] stating which right you wish to exercise. We will reply within one month. If we need to verify your identity, we will ask you.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

7. Minors

Children under 14 may not register without the consent of their parents or guardians (art. 7 LOPDGDD). You must be of legal age to buy, subscribe or donate.

8. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk: encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords never stored in plain text, role-based permissions checked on the server and in the database, a second factor for administrators, backups and a log of administrative actions.

If a security breach posed a risk to you, we would inform you and notify the Spanish Data Protection Agency within the legal deadlines.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with its date and, if the change is significant, we will let you know by email.
