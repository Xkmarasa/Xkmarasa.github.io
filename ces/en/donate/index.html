Frequently asked questions

How much can I donate?+

Choose one of the suggested amounts or enter your own, between €1.00 and €5,000.00. Each donation is one-off: there are no recurring charges.

Why do I need an account?+

We do not accept anonymous donations. With an account, the receipt goes to a confirmed email address and you can check your donations at any time.

Is the payment secure?+

Yes. You pay on Stripe's page, with encryption and your bank's verification when required. We never see or store your card details.

Will I get an invoice?+

No. A donation is not a sale, so there is no invoice or VAT. You will receive a receipt by email with the amount, date and reference.

Can I ask for a donation certificate?+

Yes, tick the box and enter your Spanish tax ID (NIF or NIE). The certificate will be issued when the association is able to do so, and we will let you know by email.
