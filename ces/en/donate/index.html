Make a donation

Your donation helps the association continue its research and outreach. It is a one-off contribution: it does not renew.

Choose an amount

You will enter it on the payment page (between €1 and €5,000).

It will appear on the receipt. We do not accept anonymous donations.

We need your Spanish tax ID (NIF or NIE). The certificate will be issued once the association is able to; we will let you know.

Apple Pay and Google Pay are confirmed on Stripe's secure page, if your device supports them.

You need an account to donate, so we can keep your receipt.

Payment is made on Stripe's secure page. You will receive a receipt by email; it is not an invoice. See the donation terms and the privacy policy.
